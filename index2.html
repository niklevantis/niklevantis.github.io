About

Nick Levantis is an electronic musician and digital artist from Piraeus, Greece.

He came of age during the fertile cultural and musical climate of the early 1980s, when programming and synthesizers drew him deeper into electronic music and computer art.

For more than four decades, he has made music and collaborated with artists across different styles, treating genre as material rather than a boundary. His recent work brings these experiences together in genre-agnostic compositions that move between structure and entropy, intimacy and abstraction, inviting the listener into unfamiliar electronic spaces.
