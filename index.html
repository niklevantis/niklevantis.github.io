Nick Levantis.

An electronic musician and digital artist from Piraeus, Greece. He came of age in the fertile climate of the early 1980s, when programming and synthesizers drew him into electronic music and computer art.

For four decades he has treated genre as material rather than boundary — compositions between structure and entropy, intimacy and abstraction.

Six rooms: the journey of reality through the transcendence of dream. Fall to go deeper. Touch the room to listen or rest. Each drawing completes as its song plays.
